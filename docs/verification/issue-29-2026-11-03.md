## Scope and remaining gates

The smoke validates integration and provenance, not ranking quality or corpus
completeness. It does not claim a full metadata crawl, full live portal/PDF/Excel
smoke, independently judged relevance improvement, final candidate acceptance
or a published v0.3.0. Those remain explicit in
[RELEASE_0.3.0.md](../RELEASE_0.3.0.md).

`--indexed-only` fails before opening stdio when no cluster URL is configured.
A configured but unavailable index fails the indexed assertion even if the tool
successfully falls back. The full smoke selects live portal search explicitly for
its table/publication checks, because those types are outside the metadata index;
the pre-existing full smoke without an index remains supported.

evidence and version/changelog work are complete. This increment changes neither
